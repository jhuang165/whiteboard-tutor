# Re-explain the sticking point

Adapted from Matt Pocock's wait-what workflow. Identify the step that failed from
the conversation; ask for its location only if it is genuinely unclear.

Restate the goal in plain language, define unfamiliar notation, and rebuild that
step with a small concrete example. For conditional probability, counts, a table,
or a reduced sample space may reveal what a formula hid. Then connect the example
back to the original expression, preserving its assumptions and edge cases.

Change the representation rather than merely repeating the explanation. Keep
the response focused and self-contained. Add a question only if the learner is
in an interactive practice session or a missing detail prevents an explanation.
